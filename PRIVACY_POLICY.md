# Privacy Policy for Pivot App

**Last updated: Septamper 2025**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pivot ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

- **Name and Contact Information**: We collect your name and email address for account creation and communication.
- **Academic Information**: We collect your department, level, and section information to provide personalized academic services.
- **Profile Information**: We collect profile images and personal descriptions you choose to share.

### Usage Information

- **App Usage**: We collect information about how you use the app, including features accessed and time spent.
- **Device Information**: We collect device information such as device type, operating system, and app version for technical support.

### Technical Information

- **Firebase Analytics**: We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns and improve user experience.
- **Push Notifications**: We collect FCM tokens to send you relevant notifications about your academic schedule and tasks.
- **Image Storage**: Profile pictures and uploaded images are stored securely in Firebase Storage with automatic compression and optimization.
- **Local Caching**: We use local device storage (Hive database) to cache profile images and app data for improved performance.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Academic Services**: To provide personalized academic content, schedule management, and task tracking.
- **Communication**: To send you important updates, notifications, and announcements.
- **App Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality.
- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and maintain account security.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except:

- **Service Providers**: We may share information with trusted service providers (Firebase, Google) who assist in app operation.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption**: All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS.
- **Secure Storage**: User data is stored securely using Firebase's security features with proper access controls.
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.
- **Image Security**: Uploaded images are automatically compressed and stored with secure URLs.
- **Modern Permissions**: We use Android's scoped storage system and only request necessary permissions (photos, camera, notifications).

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request access to your personal information.
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Portability**: Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for university students and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
